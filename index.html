Privacy Policy

PawConnect  ·  Effective date: October 28, 2026

PawConnect ("we", "our", or "the app") is a mobile app for dog owners to find playmates, organize playdates, and report lost dogs. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

Account information. When you sign in with Google or Apple we receive your email address, display name, and profile photo. Guest accounts get an anonymous identifier with no name or email.

Dog profiles. Information you enter about your dogs: name, breed, age, size, temperament, energy level, play style, vaccination status, gender, neighborhood, bio, favorite toy, and an optional photo.

Location. If you grant location permission, we use your device's current position to calculate distances between dogs and to attach a location to dog profiles, playdates, and lost-pet alerts. We do not track your location in the background — location is only read when you are actively using the app and only when you tap a "Use my current location" button.

Events and messages. Titles, descriptions, locations, and dates for playdates and pack walks you create or join. Group chat messages sent within those events.

Lost-pet alerts. Dog name, last-seen location, neighborhood, description, and optional photo for alerts you post.

Block and report actions. A list of users you have blocked (stored in your account) and any abuse reports you submit (stored separately; not visible to other users).

2. How we use your information

  • Matching your dog with compatible playmates nearby.
  • Displaying dog profiles, events, and lost-pet alerts to other users.
  • Enabling group chat within playdates you participate in.
  • Enforcing blocks — hiding content from users you have blocked.
  • Reviewing abuse reports to keep the community safe.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with analytics providers.

3. How your information is stored

All structured data (profiles, events, messages, alerts) is stored in Google Cloud Firestore. Photos are stored in Google Firebase Storage. Both services run on Google Cloud infrastructure. Access is enforced by Firestore security rules — every collection has an explicit allow-list and denies by default.

4. Who can see your information

  • Dog profiles — visible to all signed-in users.
  • Events — visible to all signed-in users; chat messages are visible only to event participants.
  • Lost-pet alerts — visible to all signed-in users.
  • Your email address — stored in your account document but not displayed to other users in the app.
  • Abuse reports — write-only from the app; not visible to any other user.
  • Blocked-users list — private to your account.

5. Your rights and controls

Edit. You can update your profile, dog profiles, events, and alerts at any time from within the app.

Delete your account. Profile → Delete Account permanently removes your user profile, all your dog profiles, all your lost-pet alerts, and all uploaded photos. Shared content you participated in (event listings, chat messages) is retained because other participants still depend on it — this mirrors standard social-app practice.

Block. Blocking a user hides their dogs from your search and matchmaking results and hides their messages in shared event chats.

Data export. Self-service data export is not yet available in the app. To request a copy of your data, contact us at the address below.

6. Children

PawConnect is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Effective date" at the top of this page reflects the date of the most recent revision. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
